The expert's quota is the scarcest resource. Let a run spend at most `expertBudget` expert tokens, counted as fresh input, cache writes and output (not cache reads), summed over the attempts made on the expert tier.

- `Preset` (`src/presets/presets.ts`) and `RunState` (`src/engine/types.ts`) get an optional `expertBudget`; preset validation keeps a positive number and otherwise leaves it unset. `StartOptions` gets an `expertBudget` that overrides the preset's, and `neuromesh run` takes `--expert-budget <tokens>` (add it to the help text). The team presets (`duo`, `trio`, `factory`, `factory-wf`) get `"expertBudget": 250000`.
- In `src/engine/run.ts`, export `expertSpend(state)`. Before an orchestrator (planning) or escalation (advice, takeover) turn, if the spend has reached the budget, do not run the turn: emit a `note` event "Expert budget reached (<spent> of <budget> tokens); no more planning in this run." (for an escalation turn, "no more expert help in this run.") and treat the turn as an error ("expert budget reached"), so planning falls back to a single worker. A stuck task that would go to advice or takeover while the budget is spent instead ends without expert help: a `note` "Expert budget reached; task <id> ends without expert help." and the task fails with its reason followed by ", and the expert budget is spent".

Graded by new tests in `test/engine.test.ts` (not in your copy of the repository). The scenario: with a budget of 1,000 tokens, one turn of advice costing 900 input and 200 output tokens spends it, so the takeover is not made, and the run finishes with failed checks and exactly one expert call.
