When a worker is stuck, the expert takes the task over from a clean start, which costs about as much as the expert doing the whole task alone. Make escalation two steps: the expert first advises, and the worker fixes.

- A preset field `advise` (boolean, default true; copied to `RunState.advise`): a stuck worker (checks keep failing, no progress, out of repair rounds, the reviewer still objects, or the turn stopped) first gets the expert's advice, and only if that fails does the expert take over as before. A task whose owner is the expert, or that was already advised, escalates directly.
- The advice step (`"advise"`, a new `TaskStep`): one read-only turn of the escalation role on the worker's current code, which must not change files, with `advisePrompt(state, task)` in `src/engine/prompts.ts`: the request (or the overall request, the task's title and brief for a part), the last two attempts as "Attempt N, its change:" with the diff (cut to 8,000 characters), "What went wrong:" (cut to 4,000, or "(checks passed)") and the reviewer's findings, and an instruction to find the real cause and give the worker the key insight and concrete steps. The answer's JSON schema `ADVICE_SCHEMA` has `diagnosis` (string) and `steps` (array of strings); `parseAdvice` returns an `Advice` (`{ diagnosis, steps }`) or undefined when the diagnosis is missing or blank, dropping blank steps. New attempt kinds: "advice" and "advised-fix".
- With usable advice: a `task.advised` event with the diagnosis and steps; one extra repair round (`repairLimit(state, task)` is `repairs` plus one once the task was advised); and the worker's next turn, of kind `advised-fix`, resumes its session with `advisedFixPrompt` ("The lead engineer looked at your work and the failures.", the diagnosis, the numbered steps, "Follow this advice, run the checks again, then give your final answer in the same JSON format as before."), or, when the session cannot be resumed, starts fresh with `freshAdvisedFixPrompt` (the task prompt, a note that an earlier attempt already changed files, and the advice). Without usable advice a note says so and the expert takes the task over.

Graded by new tests in `test/engine.test.ts` (not in your copy of the repository). The advice request is recognised by its schema having a `diagnosis` property.
