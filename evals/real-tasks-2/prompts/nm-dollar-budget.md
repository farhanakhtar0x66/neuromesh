Cap a run's expert spending in estimated dollars, keep passing work when the cap is hit, and show what each turn cost.

- Presets can set `expertBudgetUsd` (`src/presets/presets.ts`: a positive number, otherwise unset; copied to `RunState.expertBudgetUsd`): the most estimated expert spending one run may have, in US dollars at API list prices (`estimateCost(model, usage)` in `src/usage/prices.ts`; an unpriced turn counts as 0). Export `expertDollars(state)` from `src/engine/run.ts` (the estimated cost of the attempts on the expert tier). It joins the existing token budget: the expert budget is spent when either one is. The `factory-opus-luna` preset gets a budget of $1.00 and `factory-luna` $0.50.
- Before an orchestrator or escalation turn, if the dollar budget is spent, do not run it: emit a `note` event tagged `feature: "F16"` ("Expert budget reached ($X of $Y, estimated); no more planning in this run.", or "no more expert help" for an escalation turn) and treat the turn as an error, as the token budget does. When a task that is stuck would go to advice or takeover with the budget spent, but its project checks pass and its last review left findings, keep its work: a `note` tagged F16, the task goes to the `merge` step and the findings stay recorded. Otherwise it ends without expert help as before.
- Every `agent.finished` event carries `cost`: the estimate for that turn (a number; undefined when the model is unpriced). The CLI's event line shows it as `≈$0.123` after the usage text (three decimals under $0.10, else two), and `formatSummary` (`src/cli/format.ts`) adds a line `Cost:    ≈$<total> estimated at API list prices (<role> $<cost>, ...)` with the cost by role, largest first, and "; some turns unpriced" when some turn had usage but no price.
- The spec writer's prompt (`src/engine/prompts.ts`) asks for 6 to 10 features and 5 to 8 design points instead of 8 to 14 and 6 to 12, and adds the rule "Be compact: one or two sentences per point, and about 900 words in all. Every word is read by the developer; say each thing once."
- Register F15 (key `spec-cap`), F16 (`dollar-budget`) and F17 (`live-cost`), all optional and marked in the code.

Graded by new tests in `test/engine.test.ts` (not in your copy of the repository). The scenario: with a budget of $0.50 and one turn of advice that used 1,000,000 fresh input tokens of `gpt-6.1-sol` (priced $2 per million), the advice spends the budget, the task keeps its passing work, a single expert call was made, and that turn's `agent.finished` event has `cost` 2.
