Two problems from a build where the Luna tester asked for wrong letters to be shown one way and then the other, which led to an Opus takeover costing $2.34 (API estimate) for an app the project's checks already passed.

1. A reviewer does not see its earlier findings. In `testerPrompt` (`src/engine/prompts.ts`), when earlier reviews of the task left findings that are not minor (`task.history[*].findings`, severity other than "minor"), add a section after the diff: "Earlier reviews of this task asked for these, and the developer has worked on them since:", then the last 8 of them as `findingText` lines, then "Check whether they are fixed. Do not ask for the opposite of an earlier finding, and do not fail the change over a choice the request (and its spec, if any) leaves open: there, the developer's choice stands."
2. When the repair rounds are used up (`task.round >= repairLimit(state, task)`), the expert's advice has already been tried (`task.advised`), and the project's checks pass (the task's latest check results include no "failed" or "timeout", or there are none), and only the reviewer still objects, do not start an expert rebuild. Emit a `note` event "Task <id>: the checks pass and the expert's advice was applied; merging with the reviewer's remaining findings recorded." and send the task to the `merge` step.

Graded by new tests in `test/engine.test.ts` (not in your copy of the repository).
