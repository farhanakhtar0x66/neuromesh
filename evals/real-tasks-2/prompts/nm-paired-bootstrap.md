Add a paired bootstrap for comparing two configurations' benchmark results task by task, with no dependency and no model call: a new file `src/eval/stats.ts`, used by `evals/bench.ts` as `--compare`.

`src/eval/stats.ts` exports:

- `rng(seed: number): () => number`: a seeded generator (mulberry32) returning numbers in [0, 1), so the same data always gives the same interval.
- `Interval` (`{ tasks, meanA, meanB, diff, low, high, level, resamples, seed }`) and `pairedBootstrap(a: number[], b: number[], options?: { resamples?: number, level?: number, seed?: number }): Interval | undefined`. `a[i]` and `b[i]` are one task's scores (0 to 1). `diff` is the mean of `a - b`. The interval comes from drawing the tasks again with replacement `resamples` times (default 10,000) and taking each draw's mean difference; the means are sorted, and with `level` (default 0.9, two-sided) and `tail = (1 - level) / 2`, `low` is element `floor(tail * resamples)` and `high` is element `ceil((1 - tail) * resamples) - 1`. `seed` defaults to 1. Lengths that differ throw; fewer than 2 tasks returns undefined; identical results give a `diff`, `low` and `high` of 0.
- `notWorseThan(interval, margin)`: true when `interval.low > -margin` (a share: 0.1 means 10 points). `showableMargin(interval)`: `max(0, -interval.low)`.
- `taskScores(runs: Record<string, number>[]): Record<string, number>`: one configuration's runs (each maps task to 1 for pass or 0 for fail) to the mean per task over the runs that have it. `pair(a, b)`: `{ tasks, a, b }` for the tasks both have, sorted by name.
- `describeComparison(nameA, nameB, interval | undefined, margin, runsPerTask?: { a, b }): string[]`, the lines of the report. With no interval, one line: "<A> against <B>: fewer than 2 tasks in common; no interval." Otherwise a heading "<A> against <B>, paired over N tasks (runs per task: x and y)"; the two means and the mean difference in points; the interval, "90% interval for the difference: ... points (10,000 task resamples, seed 1)"; a line "not worse than <B> by more than 10 points: shown" (or "not shown"); and then one of: for a zero-width interval, a line saying it is "a ceiling" (both pass everything, or too few runs) and that it says nothing about a margin, with no further line; "<A> is better" when the whole interval is above zero; "<A> is worse" when it is below zero; otherwise that the interval holds zero; and, except for the ceiling, "with these tasks the data can show a margin of about X points, no tighter."

`node evals/bench.ts --compare a1,a2,a3 b1,b2,b3 [--margin 10] [--resamples N] [--seed N]` takes two groups of saved eval names, builds each run's task-to-pass map from `result.hidden === "pass"`, pairs the two configurations and prints `describeComparison`. Register it as F107, key `paired-bootstrap`, optional; with it off `--compare` does nothing.
