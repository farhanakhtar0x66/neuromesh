A preset can list worker rungs, cheapest first (`workerLadder`, for example one model at low effort and then at high effort). Every task starts on the first rung. When the stagnation breaker or the tester says the worker is stuck, the task climbs one rung instead of escalating: the work stays in place and a fresh session on the stronger rung is told exactly what failed. Only a stuck top rung reaches the expert (advice, then takeover as before). The engine decides every step; no model does.

- `Preset.workerLadder?: RoleConfig[]` (validated like a role chain, for the worker role); `RunState.workerLadder` (unset when the run names a worker model explicitly, which replaces the ladder); `TaskState.rung?: number` (0 is the cheapest).
- A turn request can carry `chain`: models to try instead of the role's own list. A worker turn on rung r gets the ladder from r upward; a model out of quota is skipped as in any chain.
- `canClimb(state, task)`: there is a ladder, the task is not escalated, advised or the expert's, and its rung is below the top. `onStuck(run, task, reason)`: when it can climb, `rung` goes up by one, round 0, fresh progress, `next` "climb" (a new attempt kind "climb"), no session, step "agent", and a `task.climbed` event with `from` and `to` as "backend/model" and the reason; otherwise it records the escalation reason and goes to advice or takeover as before. Use it everywhere the engine escalated a stuck worker.
- `climbPrompt(state, task)` in `src/engine/prompts.ts`: the task prompt, then "A quicker colleague attempted this task first and got stuck. Its changes are still in the files (`git diff` shows them)." followed by what went wrong: the reviewer's findings of the last attempt if it had any, else its failure text (cut to 6,000 characters), or "It stopped without finishing." when nothing was recorded; then "Finish the task correctly: keep what is right in those changes and fix or replace the rest."
- The `factory-wf` preset gets a ladder of two rungs: its worker model at low and then at high effort.

Graded by new tests in `test/engine.test.ts` (not in your copy of the repository).
