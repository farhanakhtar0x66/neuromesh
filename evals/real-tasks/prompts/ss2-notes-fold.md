The agent keeps its conversation in CONTEXT.md as a list of turns (History, capped at 40 turns by `appendTurn` in src/context.js) and a list of Notes. When the history is full, the oldest turn is simply dropped, so the model forgets what the user told it early on. Keep what falls out of the history, in short form, as Notes, and send the Notes to the model.

1. `appendTurn(state, turn, maxTurns = 40)` (src/context.js): each turn it evicts from the history is folded into Notes, appended after the existing notes, in order. A folded note is `"<role> @ <at>: <excerpt>"` (for example `user @ t1: my name is Ada`). The excerpt is the turn's content with every run of whitespace (including newlines) collapsed to one space and trimmed; when it is longer than 160 characters it is cut so the excerpt is exactly 160 characters, the last one being "…". Turns whose role is "system", and turns with no text left after trimming, are evicted without a note. Notes must survive `serializeContext` then `parseContext` unchanged (a note containing "###" is still a note).
2. `notesMessage(state)` (exported from src/context.js): null when the state has no notes; otherwise `{ role: "system", content: "Notes:\n- first\n- second" }` listing the notes in order.
3. `runTurn` (src/agent.js): the messages sent to the model are the notes message first (when there is one), then the last 20 turns of history, then nothing else. The user's new message is added to the history before the messages are made, so a turn it pushes out of the 40 is folded into the Notes the model receives: with 40 earlier user turns "fact 1" to "fact 40" at times "t1" to "t40", running "latest" leaves the state's notes as `["user @ t1: fact 1", "user @ t2: fact 2"]` (the new message and the model's reply each evict one), the history at 40, and 21 messages sent.

Tests are in test/context.test.js and test/agent.test.js.
