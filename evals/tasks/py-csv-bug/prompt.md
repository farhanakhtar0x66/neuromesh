`split_line` in `csvline.py` breaks on quoted fields: `split_line('a,"b,c",d')` returns `['a', '"b', 'c"', 'd']` instead of `['a', 'b,c', 'd']`. Fix it to follow the usual CSV rules: a field wrapped in double quotes may contain commas, a doubled quote `""` inside a quoted field means one literal quote, and the surrounding quotes are removed. Do not use the `csv` module; this file is meant to stay dependency free and readable.
