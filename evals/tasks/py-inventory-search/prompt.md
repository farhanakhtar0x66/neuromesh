Add a `search` method to `Inventory` in `inventory.py`: `search(query="", min_quantity=None, sort_by="name")`. It returns the matching `Item` objects. An item matches when `query` appears in its name, ignoring case (an empty query matches everything), and, if `min_quantity` is given, its quantity is at least that number. `sort_by` is `"name"` (A to Z, ignoring case) or `"quantity"` (highest first, ties broken by name). Any other `sort_by` raises `ValueError`. Add tests.
