# Reminders are wrong when the clocks change

Our team reminders are generated with remindly, one rule per reminder, mostly in America/New_York and Europe/London, and a
few in Australia and Chile. Since March we have had three kinds of complaints:

- Daily and weekly reminders fire an hour late after the clocks go forward (a 09:00 reminder arrives at 10:00 local
  time) and, since November, an hour early.
- A reminder set for 00:30 fired twice on 1 November, once just after midnight and again at 23:30 that evening.
- The "30th of every month" reminder never fires in February, and the "31st" one skips every short month.

Reminders in India are fine, and so is everything in January. We would also like the Lord Howe office (a 30-minute change)
and the Chatham Islands office to get the same treatment as everybody else.

Please fix remindly so that every rule fires at its local time on every day it should, in every zone.
